# Fable of the Roasted Pigs

**Source Preservation Project**

A preservation project for Gustavo F. J. Cirigliano's original *Fable of the Roasted Pigs*, created to preserve its authorship, publication history and original source as the story is frequently reproduced, adapted and circulated without attribution.

This repository preserves and republishes **“Fable of the Roasted Pigs”** by **Gustavo F. J. Cirigliano**, including a Brazilian Portuguese text and an English version, while inviting contributions for translations into additional languages.

The fable is a compact critique of organizations that become very good at managing the consequences of a broken system instead of questioning the system itself. That makes it particularly relevant to engineering, institutions, operations and public administration.

The original work is Cirigliano's; this project is about keeping the text accessible, attributed and available to a wider audience.
